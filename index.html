Events

Bring HydraGlow to you.

Weddings, weekend trips, game day, the office offsite: we show up wherever the group is and keep everyone hydrated, recovered, and running on more than caffeine and adrenaline.

For Bridal Parties

Bridal Glow, Delivered.

The week before a wedding is chaos: dress fittings, seating charts, family drama, and approximately zero sleep. We show up for the bachelorette recovery, the morning of the wedding when nerves and champagne have taken their toll, or the whole bridal party the night before so everyone walks down the aisle glowing instead of running on coffee and adrenaline. Book a single session for the bride, or turn it into an experience with the whole crew: robes on, mimosas out, IV in, getting ready together before the "I do's." We come to the hotel, the Airbnb, wherever the wedding week chaos is happening.

For Bachelor & Bachelorette Weekends

The Recovery Squad Your Group Chat Needs.

You didn't plan a whole weekend away just to spend Sunday morning stuck in a hotel bed. Whether it's a full weekend of bar crawls, a boat day, or a house rented specifically for chaos, we show up wherever the party is and get the whole group back on their feet: hydration, nausea relief, the works. Nobody has to be the one "I feel fine" person dragging everyone else through brunch. Book it for the morning after, or have us on standby for whenever the group inevitably needs it.

For Sporting Events

Game Day, Handled.

Tailgates, marathons, golf outings, fantasy draft parties, the company suite at the game: anywhere there's a crowd and a long day ahead, we'll set up shop. Pregame energy for the people running (or drinking) their way through six hours of tailgating, recovery drips for the marathoners who need to be functional the next morning, or just a steady stream of hydration keeping your group going from first pitch to last call. We bring the setup to the parking lot, the box, or the backyard. You bring the team spirit.

For Corporate & Business Events

Wellness as a Perk, Not an Afterthought.

Company offsites and conferences run people into the ground: early mornings, late dinners, too much coffee, not enough water. Bring HydraGlow in as an actual perk instead of another line item nobody uses: a wellness station at your next retreat, a recovery session after the big client dinner, or a standing option your team can book on their own. It's the kind of benefit people remember, and it beats another gift card nobody asked for.

No set pricing yet. Every event is priced individually based on group size and what you need. Tell us what you're planning and we'll build a package around it.
