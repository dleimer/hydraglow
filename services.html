How a HydraGlow appointment
actually works.

01
Step One
Book in the app — or text the line.

Studio, suite, or house call. Members get a same-day slot guaranteed within zone.

02
Step Two
Brief intake with a nurse.

Five minutes of vitals, recent labs (if any), goals for the week. Drip is then mixed in front of you.

03
Step Three
Settle in. Recline. Drift.

Eye mask, weighted blanket, a curated playlist or a hard-loaded audiobook. Hospital-grade pump, hotel-grade everything else.

04
Step Four
Glow walks out with you.

Light snack, electrolyte to go, and notes synced back to your chart. We text 36 hours later to check in.
